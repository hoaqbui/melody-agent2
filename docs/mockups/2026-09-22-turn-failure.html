When a turn fails

Today a failed turn is a toast that disappears ("Couldn't send message"). There's no Retry, and nothing shows a rate limit or a closed quota window, although PRODUCT.md §13 promises fail-over. Two states, two options each.

1 · The turn failed (network, adapter crash, auth expired)

Option 1AA card in the transcript, where the reply would have been

It stays until you act, it says what failed and what to do, and Retry re-sends the same prompt. When the cause is auth, the first button is "Sign in" instead.

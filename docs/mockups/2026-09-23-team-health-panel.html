The 1 · 1 · 2 layout
  • Sessions — Melody's pin and her companions on top; Team health is a row under them (current: 2 px teal ring), with the amber dot while anything waits
  • Chat — the dashboard, as a view like Board: five numbers, the week's note strip, memory over 8 weeks, one card per member
  • Work top — For you, a tab like any other; Work bottom — Melody's tab, where the check-in runs
Ink language (§0), unchanged
  • Teal: where you are and what's alive — the current row, running dots, progress bars, the step you're on
  • Amber: waiting on you — as a dot or a card's 3 px outline, never text
  • Green / red: outcomes only. Everything else is ink, paper and the greys
  • Companions keep their marks (Harmony, Tempo, Chord); Melody is only the app icon
History and the list
  • History sits over Melody's thread: this week's check-in, past check-ins, one-off questions about the team
  • For you splits Yours (decide inline: a charter diff, a contradiction) from Together (queued for the check-in, crossed off as she reaches them)
  • Everything reads ~/Melody/ and the companions' folders; nothing new is stored

01

The family, working

Pick

Each member's mark moves its own way while it works, so you can tell who's busy before reading a word. Melody breathes from her centre: both side bars rise together as the centre bar dips, then they settle back, so the mark stays centred and its rounded ends never squash; Harmony's two bars nod in turn, a call and its answer, never lining up (so it never reads as pause); Tempo's steps tick like a metronome; Chord's notes pulse together, then the root. The tile keeps a teal halo while working. Idle, everything is still.

02

A handoff that travels

Pick

Melody posts the card; a copy of it flies from her feed into Tempo's row in the sidebar and shrinks into it. Tempo's row takes the teal ring for a beat, its mark starts working, and its line updates. The card and the row are one session, and now you've seen it move.

card lands 180 ms · flight 560 ms ease-out · ring 900 ms · Reduce motion: both flash teal once, no flight
03

Work's panels give way

Pick

Closing the bottom panel's last tab lets the top panel slide down to fill. Closing the last tab anywhere slides Work away while Chat widens in the same beat. With no tabs, the toggle at Chat's top right slides Work back in and a new Terminal tab grows out of the toggle's corner.

180 ms ease-out, every beat · the new tab grows 180 ms from the top right · Reduce motion: cuts

09

The sent message — four ways

Where your words go when you press the send disc. Press the disc in any composer (or Replay, which resets it and presses the disc for you). Each makes the same point, that the message in the thread is the one you just wrote; they differ in what moves.

A · TravelPick
Your words lift out of the composer and fly to the thread · 560 ms
B · RisePick
Press the send disc: it dips, and your message rises out of it, up and left into its place in the thread; the composer's words fade out · 340 ms
C · LaunchPick
The disc presses; an ink dot shoots from it to where the bubble lands, and the bubble opens there · 420 ms
D · MorphPick
The composer itself shrinks into the bubble; a fresh, empty composer rises beneath it · 560 ms
all four: Reduce motion shows the bubble and clears the composer, no movement
